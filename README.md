# HR Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides an in-depth analysis of employee attrition trends. It helps identify key factors influencing employee turnover by analyzing departments, job roles, salary levels, age groups, education fields, job satisfaction, and business travel.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Active Employees: 1,233
- Attrition Count: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.1%
- Average Employee Age: 37

## 📈 Dashboard Insights
- Research & Development department has the highest attrition.
- Employees with salaries up to 5K show the highest attrition.
- Employees aged 26–35 have the highest attrition rate.
- Job roles such as Laboratory Technician and Sales Executive have significant attrition.
- Business travel frequency impacts employee attrition patterns.
